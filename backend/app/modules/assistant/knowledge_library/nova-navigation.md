# Where things are in Nova

Keywords: where, menu, find, open, navigate, sidebar, which page, where can I see, where do I, go to, di mana, menu mana, halaman, buka.

Use these exact menu names when telling someone where to go. The sidebar has these groups and pages:

- **General → Home**: the start page.
- **Data Management → Workspaces**: the SQL editor, where people write and run queries in worksheets.
- **Data Management → Database Explorer**: browse databases, tables, views and stages; upload files to a stage.
- **Data Management → Tasks**: scheduled SQL work, task graphs and their run history.
- **AI & ML → Agent**: Studio agents and their settings. **Skills** and **Tools** sit under the same group.
- **AI & ML → Semantic Views**: governed business definitions (metrics, breakdowns, verified questions); draft, review and publish them here.
- **AI & ML → Business Ontology**: shared business terms, their synonyms and the metrics they map to; stewards review proposed changes.
- **AI & ML → AI Search**, **Nova Studio**, **BI**, **Entities**, **Feature Store**, **ML Models**.
- **Access Control → Users**: people who can sign in.
- **Access Control → Roles**: roles and who has them.
- **Access Control → Data Access**: tabs **Roles**, **Data access**, **Policies** and **Check access**. On **Data access** you can let a role use a table, show a person only some rows, or hide a sensitive column. **Check access** shows what one person gets on one table with a chosen role.
- **Access Control → Audit**: who did what.
- **Administrator → AI Providers**: the AI services and models Nova uses. **Admin Settings** holds system settings.
- **Operations → Migration**.
- **Operations → Monitoring**: **AI Monitoring**, **Queries**, **Audit Trail**, **Data Loads**, **Cluster Monitor** and **Production Health**.

**Monitoring → Queries** has four tabs:
- **Running now**: queries that are running at this moment.
- **History**: queries that already ran, with status, time and errors.
- **Cost**: which queries and users use the most resources.
- **Autopilot**: Query Autopilot watches recurring queries, notices when they slow down or could be faster, tests a possible improvement safely, and suggests it. A suggestion is only a proposal: nothing changes until an authorized person reviews and approves it. Nothing needs to be turned on to read its findings.

The role picker at the bottom left of the sidebar sets the one active role; what you can see and do on every page follows that role.
