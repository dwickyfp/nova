## What it is

Every user has a small profile and a few personal choices. They affect only
you.

## Open your settings

1. Click your name at the bottom of the left menu.
2. Click **Settings**. The **Settings** page opens on **Profile**.

## Update your profile

The **Profile** page shows:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| **Profile picture** | Your initials, made from your first and last name. |
| **Username** | Your sign-in name. You cannot change it here. |
| **First name**, **Last name** | Up to 100 characters each. |
| **Email** | Must be a valid email address. |

1. Change **First name**, **Last name**, or **Email**.
2. Click **Save changes**. Nova shows *Profile saved.* To undo your edits
   before saving, click **Cancel**.

An email that is not a valid address is not saved. If saving fails, the page
shows *Could not save your profile. Please try again.*

## Choose light or dark mode

1. Click your name at the bottom of the left menu.
2. Point at **Appearance**.
3. Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** (follow your computer's setting).

The choice is remembered in this browser.

## Change your password

Nova asks you to set a new password when your administrator requires it. See
[Sign in and choose your role](/docs/getting-started/sign-in-and-roles#set-a-new-password-when-nova-asks).
If you forgot your password, ask an administrator to reset it. See
[Users](/docs/access-control/users#reset-a-password).

## See your account and connection details

Click your name, then **Information**. See
[Find your way around](/docs/getting-started/navigating#account-details).
