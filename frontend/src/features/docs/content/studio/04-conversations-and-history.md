---
title: Conversations and history
description: Find past conversations, rename or delete them, and see what an agent remembers.
---

## What it is

Every question you send starts or continues a **conversation** (also called a
chat). Nova keeps your conversations so you can come back to an answer, its
charts, and the steps behind it later. Only you see your conversations.

## Find a past conversation

1. In Studio, open the left rail. If you only see icons, click
   **Expand sidebar** at the top.
2. Under **History**, conversations are listed newest first, with the date.
   Nova names each one from its first question.
3. Click a conversation to open it. The agent it was held with is selected
   again, and you can continue asking.
4. If the list is long, scroll down and click **Load more conversations**.

## Rename a conversation

1. Point at the conversation in **History** and click **...**
   (**Actions for** the conversation).
2. Choose **Rename**.
3. Type the new name and press **Enter**.

## Delete a conversation

1. Point at the conversation in **History** and click **...**.
2. Choose **Delete**.

The conversation and its answers are removed from your history.

> **Warning:** Delete does not ask for confirmation and there is no undo.

## Start a new conversation

Click **New chat** at the top of the rail. Use a new conversation when you
change subject, so the agent does not carry assumptions from the earlier
questions.

## Agent memory

An agent can remember lasting business rules you explain to it during a
conversation, such as "our fiscal year starts in April".

1. Click your name at the bottom of the rail.
2. Choose **Memory**.

The **Agent memory** window lists the facts the selected agent remembers from
your conversations under your **current role**. Each account and agent has its
own memories. When there are none it says "No memories yet".

## Limits

- Questions you ask the Dataviz assistant do not appear in this **History**
  list.
