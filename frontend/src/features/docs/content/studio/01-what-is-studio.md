## What it is

**Nova Studio** is where you ask questions about your company's data in plain
language and get answers, tables, and charts back. Behind each answer is an
**agent**: an assistant that an administrator has set up for one area of the
business (for example sales, stock, or HR) and connected to the data that area
uses.

Studio never gives an agent more access than you have. Every number comes from
a query that runs with **your** sign-in and your **active role** (the role
shown under your name). If your role may not see a table, or sees only some of
its rows, the agent sees exactly the same.

## Before you start

- You need a Nova account. Sign in as usual.
- At least one agent must exist and be shared with you. If Studio shows no
  agent, ask your administrator, or create one under **Agents** if you are
  allowed to.

## Open Studio

1. In the Console's left menu, choose **Nova Studio**.
2. Studio opens in a **new browser tab**, so the page you were on stays open.

Studio remembers the agent you last talked to in this browser and opens it
again next time.

## The parts of the screen

At the top of Studio is a switch with two tabs:

| Tab | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Agent** | Chat with one agent at a time. Answers can contain text, tables, charts and maps. |
| **Dataviz** | Build and view charts and dashboards, with an assistant that answers questions about the dashboard on screen. See [What is Dataviz?](/docs/dataviz/what-is-dataviz). |

On the **Agent** tab the left rail holds:

| Item | What it opens |
| --- | --- |
| **New chat** | An empty conversation. |
| **News** | The newsroom of material business changes (when News is switched on for you). See [News and alerts](/docs/studio/news-and-alerts). |
| **Artifacts** | Your saved charts and dashboards. See [Artifacts](/docs/studio/artifacts). |
| **Capabilities** | The skills and connectors your agents can use. See [Capabilities](/docs/studio/capabilities). |
| **History** | Your past conversations, newest first. See [Conversations and history](/docs/studio/conversations-and-history). |

Click the panel button at the top of the rail (**Collapse sidebar** /
**Expand sidebar**) to hide or show the names. When the rail is collapsed only
the icons stay, which gives charts and editors more room.

## Your account menu

Click your name at the bottom of the rail to open the account menu:

- **Switch role**: choose which of your roles is active. Answers, artifacts and
  dashboards follow the active role.
- **Appearance**: light, dark, or follow your system.
- **Memory**: see the facts the selected agent remembers about your work
  (see [Conversations and history](/docs/studio/conversations-and-history)).
- **Sign out**.

## Limits

- Studio runs one agent per question. To use a different agent, pick it in the
  message box before you send.
- Answers from the AI model take time: a typical question takes from about
  half a minute to a minute.
