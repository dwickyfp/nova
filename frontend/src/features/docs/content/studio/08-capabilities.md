## What it is

The **Capabilities** page lists what your agents can draw on besides your
data:

- **Skills**: written instructions that teach an agent a repeatable piece of
  work, such as how your team builds a monthly sales review.
- **MCP Connectors**: connections to other internal tools, managed by your
  administrator.

## Open Capabilities

1. In Studio's left rail, click **Capabilities**.
2. Choose **Skills** or **MCP Connectors** on the left.

## Skills

**Your skills** lists the skills you own. Use **Search** to find one by name.

To add a skill, click **Create** and choose **Create with chat** (write the
skill together with Nova in a conversation) or **Upload a skill** (add a
skill you already wrote).

When you have no skills yet, the page shows **Get started with skills** with a
**Create a skill** card.

## MCP Connectors

This tab lists the internal connectors your administrator has made available
("Internal MCP connectors managed by your administrator"). You cannot add
connectors here.

## Limits

- Connectors are listed here for reference; only an administrator manages
  them.
