title: News and alerts
description: Understand Studio's News page, who can see it, and where monitor alerts appear.
---

## What it is

**News** is a newspaper-style page in Studio that reports material changes in
the business, drawn only from the Semantic Views your role can read.

**Monitor alerts** is the alerts desk next to it: material changes with the
evidence and investigation behind them.

## Before you start

News is a managerial page, so it is **off** for every user until an
administrator switches it on for that user. The setting is per user, under
**Users & Roles** in the Console. Ask your administrator if you need it.

News access never widens data access: it shows only changes in data your
active role may read.

## Open News

1. In Studio's left rail, click **News**.
2. If News is on for you, the latest edition opens. The refresh button at the
   top right reloads it.

If News is not switched on for you, the page says "News is not enabled for
your account".

## Open the alerts desk

1. On the **News** page, click **Monitor alerts**.
2. The desk opens, with **Manage monitors** at the top.

Without News access, the desk shows "This item is unavailable with your
current access" and "News is not enabled for this account".

## Limits

- Only an administrator can switch News on or off for a user.
- News and alerts cover only the Semantic Views your active role can read.
