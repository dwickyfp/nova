## The Home page

**Home** is the first page after you sign in. It has four parts:

| Part | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Search Nova objects and docs** | Type to filter your recent work and templates, and to find matching documentation pages. |
| **Quick actions** | **New SQL query**, **Upload files to stage**, **Browse databases**, and **Manage users**. |
| **Recent work** | Your recent SQL queries and saved worksheets. The **All** and **Queries** tabs filter the list. Click a row to open it in a worksheet. |
| **Start with a template** | Ready-made examples with sample data. Click one to open it in a worksheet, then edit and run it. |

The **New query** button at the top of the page opens an empty worksheet.

## The left menu

The left menu lists every area of Nova. A small arrow next to an item, such as
**AI & ML** or **Monitoring**, means it opens a group of pages. **Nova Studio**
opens in a new browser tab so the page you were on stays open.

See [Welcome to Nova](/docs/getting-started/welcome) for what each item is for.

## Your account menu

Click your name at the bottom of the left menu. The menu shows your username
and your active role, and has these items:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Switch Role** | Work under another of your roles. See [Sign in and choose your role](/docs/getting-started/sign-in-and-roles). |
| **Appearance** | Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** (follow your computer's setting). |
| **Information** | Opens **Account details**. See below. |
| **Docs** | Opens this documentation in a new browser tab. |
| **Settings** | Opens your profile. See [Your settings](/docs/administration/your-settings). |
| **Sign out** | Ends your session after you confirm. |

### Account details

**Information** opens a window with three tabs:

- **Account**: your **Login name**, **Active role**, **Available roles**, the
  **Console URL**, and the SQL engine and client protocol Nova uses.
- **Connection**: the **Host** and **Port** to connect a MySQL-compatible SQL
  client (such as the `mysql` command-line tool or DBeaver) to Nova. You sign in
  there with the same Nova username and password.
- **SQL commands**: a ready-to-copy command for your terminal, and an example
  that reads a file from a stage.

Each value has a copy button next to it.
